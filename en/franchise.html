The journey, in six steps

The order matters: premises and licence come before training. An authority refusing premises after training has wasted both.

1

Application

The form at the bottom of this page. Target city, situation, personal contribution, target date.

2

Interview and file

An interview, then the pre-contractual disclosure document: what the network provides, what it charges, what it requires.

3

Territory study

Number of young children, existing supply, commutes. A territory that cannot carry a nursery shows up before the lease, not after.

4

Premises and licence

Finding the premises, bringing them up to standard, filing with the authority. This is the longest step, and its length is not ours to set.

5

Training

The method, the tools, day-to-day management, working with families. The manager and the first practitioner attend together.

6

Assisted opening

Someone from the network is on site for the first days. The first enrolments are handled together.

No durations are given: step 4 depends on the lead time of the licensing authority, and therefore on the country. It will appear here once the country is settled.

Who brings what

A franchise is judged on this page, not on a brochure.

The network brings

  • The brand and its guidelines
  • The written method and materials
  • Initial training and annual refresher
  • The software: enrolment, attendance, billing, scheduling
  • The territory study before the lease is signed
  • Group purchasing: furniture, equipment, consumables
  • The network site and the nursery's local page
  • Quality control and site visits
  • Exclusivity over a territory defined in the contract

The franchisee brings

  • The premises, and bringing them up to standard
  • The personal contribution and the financing
  • Recruiting the team, with the network's help
  • Their presence: this is not an investment, it is a job
  • Applying the method and the standards
  • The relationship with families and the local authority

Questions

Do I need an early-years qualification to open a KIMO?

To RUN the facility, national law almost always requires one, and that is what decides, not the network. A candidate without it opens with a manager who has it. The exact qualification is one of the points to be settled once the country is chosen.

How many places per nursery?

That is a regulatory ceiling, not a commercial choice: the number that defines a micro-nursery is set nationally. It will appear here as soon as the country of the first opening is settled.

What is the entry fee?

It has not been set yet. Until it is, this page shows "to be set" rather than a ballpark: a candidate builds their financing on that number.

Does the network take equity in my company?

No. A franchise is not a subsidiary: the franchisee owns their company, their lease and their business. The network licenses the use of a brand and a method.

Can I open several nurseries?

That is the point of the model: small units, therefore several. In practice the second is signed after a full first year, with the licence obtained and a stable team.

What exactly is exclusive?

A territory defined in the contract, not a whole city as a matter of course. Its size is set from the number of young children living there, which the territory study measures.

Does the network help with financing?

It provides the file: territory study, forecast, description of the concept. It does not lend and does not act as guarantor.

What happens if the licence is refused?

That is why premises and licence come BEFORE training in the journey, and why the contract must address the case explicitly. The exact wording belongs to the legal file to be drawn up in the chosen country.
